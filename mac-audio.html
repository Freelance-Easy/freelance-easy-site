From your line items to a finished PDF.

  1. Install and sign in with Google.

    Choose the folder where your invoice data will live. That folder is the whole database.

  2. Add the work you're billing for.

    Your client and the line items — "Mix — 'Slow Water'", "Engineer day rate", "Kit fee — preamps + mics". Pick one of four templates, add your logo, save the PDF. Your first invoice is free.

  3. Send it to the artist, label or producer.

    Attach the PDF to an email yourself, or set up your own email account in the app and send from there. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

The 60 seconds is the invoice itself, after installation and sign-in. Setting up email sending is a separate, optional step.

Retainers, overdue clients, and the invoices you've already sent.

  • Monthly retainers generate themselves on the day you set — a podcast network's four episodes a month, say. You review the invoice and send it; nothing sends by itself.
  • Overdue reminders. See who's late and open a drafted follow-up email to review before sending.
  • Collected, outstanding, overdue — the totals and a revenue chart for the last twelve months.
  • Your old invoices. Import the PDFs you sent before so your numbering carries on.

Before you download.

Where are my invoices stored?
In a database file on your Mac, in a folder you choose. Freelance Easy doesn't receive or host it, so back that folder up the way you back up your sessions. There's no cloud sync.
Why do I sign in with Google?
Google sign-in identifies your account (your email and name). The app checks your subscription with our server once each time it starts. Your invoice database stays on your Mac.
How do I email an invoice?
Save the PDF and attach it to any email, or set up your own email account's outgoing-mail settings (SMTP) in the app and send from there. Either way the message comes from your address.
Can clients pay through it?
No. There are no online payment links. Your clients pay you the way they do now and you mark the invoice paid. US dollars only.
How do I stop using it?
Cancel through the Stripe billing portal in Settings; your files stay on your Mac. To close your account entirely, email privacy@freelance-easy.com.
