Invoicing for mix engineers, producers and studios, on your Mac.

Make your first invoice in 60 seconds

Your first invoice is free. No card, no time limit. When you need more, it's $5 a month or $50 a year.

After installing and signing in with Google, a new invoice is a client, a few line items and a PDF: a day rate, a kit fee, a per-song mix. Your invoice database stays on your Mac.

Download for Mac Need the Windows version?
For Apple Silicon Macs, M1 or later. Intel Macs aren't supported. The app is signed and notarized by Apple.

Open the Apple menu, then About This Mac. It names either an Apple chip (M1, M2, M3, M4…) or an Intel processor.

Making an invoice

  1. 1New invoice

    The number and the date are filled in for you. Add a title, pick the payment terms, and choose the client, or add a new one right there.

  2. 2The work

    One line for each thing you're billing: description, rate, quantity. Descriptions you've used before are suggested as you type, and the totals update as you go. Add a note if you want one.

  3. 3Done

    Create Invoice opens the finished invoice in the template and accent color you chose, with your logo. Save the PDF, or email it from your own address.

Keeping track

The dashboard shows what's been collected, what's outstanding and what's overdue, with a revenue chart for the last twelve months. An overdue invoice comes with a drafted follow-up email that you review before it goes anywhere.

For a retainer, set up a recurring invoice. It's generated on schedule and waits for you to review and send it; nothing is sent by itself. You can also keep client records and import the invoices you sent before from their PDFs, so your numbering carries on.

A few things to know

Where is my data?
In a database file on your Mac, in a folder you choose. Freelance Easy doesn't receive or host it, so back that folder up with your sessions. There's no cloud sync.
Why Google sign-in?
It identifies your account by your name and email. The app checks your subscription with our server each time it starts; the invoices themselves stay on your Mac.
Can clients pay through it?
No. There are no online payment links. Clients pay you the way they do now, and you mark the invoice paid.

Freelance Easy is a desktop app for one person, in US dollars. There's no mobile app, no sync between computers and no team features. To close your account, email privacy@freelance-easy.com.
