What's inside

A small tool that does the boring parts well.

Three things matter when you invoice clients: that your data stays yours, that your PDFs look like they came from someone who cares, and that recurring work doesn't cost you another Tuesday morning.

Local-first.

Your invoices, clients, and PDFs live on your machine. No SaaS lock-in, no monthly cloud bill for storage, no surprise migration when a startup pivots. Back up to your own Dropbox, Time Machine, or a USB stick.

SQLite · file-based · portable

Beautiful PDFs.

Four typeset templates designed for freelance work — bold, classic, minimal, modern (with more to come). Real serifs, real spacing, real margins. None of the Word-export ugliness that makes clients wonder if you're a real business.

4 templates · more on the way · your logo

Recurring + reminders.

Set a monthly retainer once; it sends itself on the day you choose. Get nudged when a client goes overdue, with a ready-to-send follow-up email already drafted in your voice. No CRM bolted on top.

Schedules · auto-send · gentle nudges

Native installers

One-click setup on Windows and macOS.

Your accent, your fonts

Pick the color and serif for your invoices. They'll match your brand, not ours.

Light + dark

The app remembers what you like. So does this page.
