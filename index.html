Making an invoice

The job's done and it's time to bill it: from a new invoice to a PDF ready to send, in about a minute.

  1. 1

  3. 3

Keeping track

An invoice is outstanding until the money arrives and you mark it paid. The dashboard shows what came in, what's still out and what's overdue.

An overdue invoice comes with a drafted follow-up email. A monthly retainer can be a recurring invoice, generated on schedule. If you're moving from another tool, import the invoices you've already sent from their PDFs, so your numbering carries on.

Payment details

Checkout is handled by Stripe: card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or Link. There's a field for promotion codes at checkout.

You're in control

Your files stay with you.
Your clients and invoices are a database file in a folder you choose; back it up with the rest of your work. Freelance Easy doesn't host it or sync it anywhere.
You decide what gets sent.
Invoices go out from your own email address. Recurring invoices and overdue follow-ups are drafted for you and wait for your OK.
Clients pay you directly.
There are no payment links. Clients pay you the way they do now, and you mark the invoice paid.
Sign-in and account details

Signing in with Google identifies your account by name and email, and the app checks your subscription with our server when it starts; the invoices themselves stay on your computer. No team features, no mobile app. To close your account, email privacy@freelance-easy.com.
