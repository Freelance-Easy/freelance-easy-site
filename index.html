From your line items to a finished PDF.

  1. Install and sign in with Google.

    Choose the folder where your invoice data will live. That folder is the whole database.

  2. Add your details, your client, and the work.

    Line items, rates, quantities. Pick one of four templates, add your logo and accent color, save the PDF. Your first invoice is free.

  3. Send it to your client.

    Attach the PDF to an email yourself, or set up your own email account in the app and send from there. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

The 60 seconds is the invoice itself, after installation and sign-in. Setting up email sending is a separate, optional step.

Keep track of invoices after you send them.

  • Collected, outstanding, overdue — the totals and a revenue chart for the last twelve months.
  • Recurring invoices. Set a schedule for repeat work — a monthly retainer, say — and the invoice is generated on that day. You review it and send it; recurring invoices never send by themselves.
  • Overdue reminders. See who's late and open a drafted follow-up email to review before sending.
  • Clients and old invoices. Keep client records, and import the invoices you sent before from their PDFs so your numbering carries on.

Before you download.

Where are my invoices stored?
In a database file on your computer, in a folder you choose. Freelance Easy doesn't receive or host it, so back that folder up the way you back up your other work. There's no cloud sync.
Why do I sign in with Google?
Google sign-in identifies your account (your email and name). The app checks your subscription with our server once each time it starts. Your invoice database stays on your computer.
How do I email an invoice?
Save the PDF and attach it to any email, or set up your own email account's outgoing-mail settings (SMTP) in the app and send from there. Either way the message comes from your address.
Can clients pay through it?
No. There are no online payment links. Your clients pay you the way they do now — bank transfer, Zelle, PayPal, check — and you mark the invoice paid.
Phone, sync, teams, currencies?
Desktop only, one computer, one person, US dollars. There's no mobile app, no sync between computers and no shared team features.
How do I stop using it?
Cancel through the Stripe billing portal in Settings; your files stay on your computer. To close your account entirely, email privacy@freelance-easy.com.
