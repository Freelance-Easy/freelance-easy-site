Straight answers

What it does — and what it doesn't.

Where is my data?
On your computer, in a folder you choose. We don't receive or host your invoice database. Back it up the way you back up anything else.
What does talk to your server?
Once per launch the app checks your subscription with our server — your Google account email and whether you're paid up. That's it. A bug report reaches us only if you choose to send one.
How do invoices reach my clients?
From your own email account, as a PDF — or save the PDF and send it however you like. Nothing sends without you.
Can clients pay through it?
Not yet. There are no payment links; you get paid the way you do now — bank transfer, Zelle, PayPal, check — and mark the invoice paid.
Which currencies?
US dollars only, today.
Mac and Windows?
Mac: Apple Silicon (M1 or later), signed and notarized by Apple. Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit — the installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows shows a warning the first time you run it.
