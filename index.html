Make your first invoice in 60 seconds

Your first invoice is free. No card, no time limit. When you need more, it's $5 a month or $50 a year.

After installing and signing in with Google, a new invoice is a client, a few line items and a PDF. Your invoice database stays on your computer.

Making an invoice

Add your client and the work you're billing for, with a rate and a quantity on each line. Choose one of four templates, add your logo and accent color, and save the PDF.

Attach it to an email, or connect your own email account in the app (its outgoing-mail settings, SMTP) and send from there. Either way the message comes from your address.

Keeping track

The dashboard shows what's been collected, what's outstanding and what's overdue, with a revenue chart for the last twelve months. An overdue invoice comes with a drafted follow-up email that you review before it goes anywhere.

For a monthly retainer, set up a recurring invoice. It's generated on schedule and waits for you to review and send it; nothing is sent by itself. You can also keep client records and import the invoices you sent before from their PDFs, so your numbering carries on.

A few things to know

Where is my data?
In a database file on your computer, in a folder you choose. Freelance Easy doesn't receive or host it, so back that folder up with the rest of your work. There's no cloud sync.
Why Google sign-in?
It identifies your account by your name and email. The app checks your subscription with our server each time it starts; the invoices themselves stay on your computer.
Can clients pay through it?
No. There are no online payment links. Clients pay you the way they do now, and you mark the invoice paid.

Freelance Easy is a desktop app for one person, in US dollars. There's no mobile app, no sync between computers and no team features. To close your account, email privacy@freelance-easy.com.
