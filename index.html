You're in control.

  • Your files stay with you.

    Your clients and invoices are a database file in a folder you choose; back it up with the rest of your work. Freelance Easy doesn't host it or sync it anywhere.

  • From your own address.

    Invoices go out from your own email address.

  • You decide what gets sent.

    Recurring invoices are drafted for you and wait for your OK.

  • Clients pay you directly.

    There are no payment links. Clients pay you the way they do now, and you mark the invoice paid.

Sign-in and account details

Signing in with Google identifies your account by name and email, and the app checks your subscription with our server when it starts; the invoices themselves stay on your computer. No team features, no mobile app. To close your account, email privacy@freelance-easy.com.
