Download for Mac Need the Windows version?
For Apple Silicon Macs, M1 or later, running macOS 12 Monterey or later. Intel Macs aren't supported. The app is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Freelance Easy is in beta.
Check your Mac's chip and macOS version

Open the Apple menu, then About This Mac. It names either an Apple chip (M1, M2, M3, M4…) or an Intel processor, and shows your macOS version.

This page is for the Mac version. The Windows download is on the main page.

Installing: drag Freelance Easy into Applications, then sign in with Google. Your clients and invoices are saved in a folder you choose on your Mac. Each time it starts, the app checks your subscription with our server and looks for updates; an update downloads in the background and installs when you choose to restart. Installation help

There's no mobile app. Send yourself the link and open it on your Mac.

Making an invoice

The job's done and it's time to bill it: from a new invoice to a PDF ready to send, in about a minute.

  1. 1

    New invoice. The number and date are filled in; pick the client and the terms.

  2. 2

    Line items. Description, rate, quantity; the totals update as you type.

  3. 3

    Create Invoice. Set up your email account in Settings to send it from your own address, or save the PDF and send it yourself.

Keeping track

An invoice is outstanding until the money arrives and you mark it paid. The dashboard shows what came in, what's still out and what's overdue.

The dashboard also flags invoices due within three days, so you can follow up in time. A monthly retainer can be a recurring invoice. Due invoices are generated when you open the app; you review and send their emails yourself. PDF import supports a limited set of text-based invoice layouts with numbers such as INV1001. Check the imported details; automatic numbering continues only when the imported numbers match your format in Settings.

Payment details

Checkout is handled by Stripe: card, Apple Pay or Link. There's a field for promotion codes at checkout. Stripe keeps your card details; we never see them.

You're in control

Your files stay with you.
Your clients and invoices are a database file in a folder you choose; back it up with the rest of your work. Freelance Easy doesn't host it, and the app never uploads it to us.
You decide what gets sent.
Invoices go out from your own email address. Recurring invoices are drafted for you and wait for your OK.
Clients pay you directly.
There are no payment links. Clients pay you the way they do now, and you mark the invoice paid.
Sign-in and account details

Signing in with Google identifies your account by name and email, and the app checks your subscription with our server when it starts; the invoices themselves stay on your Mac. No team features, no mobile app. To close your account, email privacy@freelance-easy.com.
