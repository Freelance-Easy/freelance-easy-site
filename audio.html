How it works

Sixty seconds is the second step.

  1. 1

    Download and open it.

    About a minute. On a Mac it's signed and notarized by Apple, so it just opens. Pick a folder for your data — that's where everything lives.

  2. 2

    Sign in with Google and make your first invoice.

    Free, no card. Your studio's details, the client's, the line items — "Mix — 'Slow Water' × 5", "Engineer day rate", "Kit fee" — about sixty seconds to a finished PDF.

  3. 3

    Send it from your own email.

    The PDF goes to the artist, label or producer from your email account; we never see it. When you need the next invoice, it's $5 a month or $50 a year — and everything you made stays yours either way.

Straight answers

What it does — and what it doesn't.

Where is my data?
On your computer, in a folder you choose. We don't receive or host your invoice database. Back it up the way you back up your sessions.
What does talk to your server?
Once per launch the app checks your subscription with our server — your Google account email and whether you're paid up. That's it.
Can clients pay through it?
Not yet. No payment links — you get paid the way you do now and mark the invoice paid. US dollars only, today.
Retainers and recurring sessions?
Set the schedule once; the invoice generates itself on the day you choose, drafted and ready to send. Overdue clients get a nudge on your dashboard with a follow-up email already written. Nothing sends without you.
