Privacy policy.

A real privacy policy will be published with the public launch.

Short version while we're in beta: Freelance Easy stores your invoices, clients, and settings on your own machine. We don't collect, sell, or transmit your invoice data. Crash logs and anonymous license heartbeats are the only things that touch our servers, and only when you choose to share them.

Questions? Privacy-specific concerns go to privacy@freelance-easy.com. Everything else, support@freelance-easy.com.
