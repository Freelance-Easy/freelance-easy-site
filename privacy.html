Privacy policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026 · Effective the same day.

The short version. Your invoices, clients and PDFs are stored on your own computer. We don't receive or host your invoice database. What we keep is what your account needs: the email and name from your Google sign-in, your subscription status, and a few technical details. Our website uses cookieless analytics. We don't sell personal information, and we share it only with the providers listed below that we need to run the service.

1. Who we are

Freelance Easy is an invoicing app made by Freelance Easy LLC ("we", "us"). It lets freelancers keep track of clients, make invoices, send them by email, and keep everything in a folder on their own machine. Privacy questions: privacy@freelance-easy.com. Security reports: security@freelance-easy.com.

2. Where your data lives

Freelance Easy is a local-first app. Your business data stays on your computer, not on our servers — a deliberate design choice that keeps your clients' information out of our hands. We do not host your invoice database, so we cannot leak it; we also cannot restore it for you, so keep a backup.

On your computer (never on our servers)

  • All invoices, line items, payment terms, notes and PDFs
  • All client information (names, emails, addresses, phone numbers)
  • Attachments, documents, logos and imported invoices
  • Your email settings and the SMTP password you enter to send invoices from your own email account (stored encrypted on your machine)
  • Your recurring schedules and, if you turned it on, your backup folder

On our servers

  • Your email address, display name and account identifier from Google, received when you sign in
  • Your subscription status and its expiry date, plus your Stripe customer identifier and the notifications Stripe sends us about your subscription (started, renewed, failed, cancelled)
  • The date your account was created
  • A secret used to verify that requests come from your copy of the app
  • Standard server request logs (IP address, request path, time), kept briefly by our hosting provider
  • Crash reports from our server (not from the app on your computer), with personal information removed before they are sent to our error-tracking service
  • A bug report, only if you choose to send one from inside the app

The app checks your subscription with our server once each time it starts, tied to your account. That check carries your account identifier and the app version; it does not carry your invoices, your clients or how many invoices you've made.

3. Our website

freelance-easy.com uses Plausible Analytics, a cookieless, privacy-focused service. It counts which pages are viewed, the referring site or campaign, the browser, operating system and country, and which download button was clicked — in aggregate, so we can see which campaigns bring visitors. It sets no cookies. To tell visits apart within a day it hashes your IP address and browser details into an identifier that is discarded every day; the IP address itself is not stored (see Plausible's data policy). The analytics script is served through our own domain. We do not use advertising pixels, retargeting, or any third-party tracker, and nothing on this website is linked to what you do inside the app.

4. Why we collect what we collect

  • To let you sign in. Google handles the password; we receive your email and name after you authorize it. The app requests only your basic profile (name, email).
  • To charge your subscription. Stripe handles the payment and your card details; we receive a customer identifier and a notification when your subscription changes. We never see your full card number.
  • To run the service securely. Server logs and server-side crash reports help us find and fix problems and detect abuse.
  • To communicate about your account. Receipts, payment failures, security notices and service announcements go to your account email. Tips or product news only if you opt in (nothing of the kind is sent today), and every such email would have a one-click unsubscribe.
  • To understand which channels work. The campaign parameters on our website are used only as aggregate counts.

Under the GDPR our lawful bases are performance of contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) for your account and subscription, and legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) for security logs and aggregate analytics.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your account data: for as long as you have an account. To close your account, email privacy@freelance-easy.com from the address you signed in with; we erase it from our live systems and it leaves our hosting provider's short-lived backups on their schedule.
  • Server request logs and server crash reports: kept briefly, then deleted.
  • Your local data (invoices, clients, attachments, backup folder): under your control. We never delete it from your computer.

You are responsible for keeping the invoice records your tax law requires — most jurisdictions want several years of them. Deleting an invoice in the app deletes it from your computer; we never had a copy.

7. International transfers

Our servers are in the United States. If you are outside the U.S., your account data is processed there. We rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, as applicable. Our documentation and support are oriented toward U.S. and Canadian users.

8. Your rights

  • Access and portability — email us and we'll send you a copy of everything we hold about your account. Your invoices and clients are already on your computer, in an ordinary SQLite database and PDF files.
  • Correction — edit anything in the app; for account fields you can't edit, email us.
  • Deletion — delete invoices and clients in the app at any time; email us to close your account.
  • Objection — email privacy@freelance-easy.com to object to any processing.

California (CCPA/CPRA): you also have the right to know the categories of personal information we collect (§2), the right to opt out of sale or sharing (we don't sell or share), and the right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights. Residents of other U.S. states with privacy laws have equivalent rights. EU/UK/EEA: you may also complain to your supervisory authority. Canada: you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner (priv.gc.ca); Quebec residents may complain to the Commission d'accès à l'information. We don't use automated decision-making.

9. Children

Freelance Easy is a business tool and is not intended for anyone under 16. If you believe someone under 16 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

10. Security

  • All traffic between the app, this website and our server uses HTTPS.
  • Subscription and license requests are signed, so a stolen account identifier alone can't be used to act as you.
  • Our hosting provider encrypts database backups at rest.
  • Your local data is stored on your own computer — its security is ultimately in your hands. We recommend full-disk encryption (FileVault on macOS, BitLocker on Windows) and a backup.

If we learn of a personal-data breach affecting you, we will notify you within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

11. Your backup folder (if you use it)

The backup folder is optional and off by default. When on, the app writes copies of your invoice PDFs and attachments to a folder you choose. It's your folder: deleting an invoice in the app does not remove the copy there, and if you chose a synced folder (Dropbox, iCloud Drive, OneDrive), your sync software will carry those files to your cloud account under your terms with that provider.

12. Changes

Material changes are posted here at least 30 days before they take effect, with a notice in the app. Minor edits (typos, an address, a replacement provider of the same kind) happen without notice.
