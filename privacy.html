Privacy policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026 · Effective the same day.

The short version. Your invoices, clients and PDFs are stored on your own computer. We don't receive or host your invoice database. What we keep is what your account needs: the email and name from your Google sign-in, your subscription and payment records, and a few technical details — plus how you heard about us and whether you want tips by email, if you tell us. Our website runs no analytics today. We don't sell personal information, and we share it only with the providers listed below that we need to run the service.

1. Who we are

Freelance Easy is an invoicing app made by Freelance Easy LLC ("we", "us"). It lets freelancers keep track of clients, make invoices, send them by email, and keep everything in a folder on their own machine. Privacy questions: privacy@freelance-easy.com. Security reports: security@freelance-easy.com.

2. Where your data lives

Freelance Easy is a local-first app. Your business data stays on your computer, not on our servers — a deliberate design choice that keeps your clients' information out of our hands. We do not host your invoice database, so we cannot leak it; we also cannot restore it for you, so keep a backup.

On your computer (never on our servers)

  • All invoices, line items, payment terms, notes and PDFs
  • All client information (names, emails, addresses, phone numbers)
  • Attachments, documents, logos and imported invoices
  • Your email settings and the SMTP password you enter to send invoices from your own email account (kept in the app's database on your computer, not encrypted)
  • Your recurring schedules and, if you turned it on, your backup folder

On our servers

  • Your email address, display name and account identifier from Google, received when you sign in
  • Your subscription status and its expiry date, kept up to date from the notifications Stripe sends us (started, renewed, failed, cancelled); your Stripe customer and subscription identifiers; and the promotion code, if you used one at checkout
  • A record of each checkout you open (the date, the plan and the app version) and of each payment that goes through (the amount, currency, plan and date), with Stripe's references for them. Each record also carries the week your account was created and your answer to "how did you hear about us", if you gave one.
  • The date your account was created
  • When your free trial started, so the app can tell when it ends (one trial per account)
  • Your answers from setting up the app, if you gave them: how you heard about us (one choice from a fixed list) and whether you ticked the box for occasional tips by email. They reach us only when you open checkout, together with which plan the app showed you first. We keep the first answers we receive, and the date the tips box reached us; an unticked box sends nothing.
  • A secret used to verify that requests come from your copy of the app
  • Server logs, kept briefly by our hosting provider: the request log (IP address, request path, time) and our server's own log, which notes your account identifier with events such as signing in, subscription checks, checkouts and payments
  • Crash reports from our server (not from the app on your computer). Your email address and name are removed before a report is sent to our error-tracking service, but it can still include your account identifier and the request that failed — for a bug report, its text.
  • A bug report, only if you choose to send one from inside the app. It holds the subject and description you type, the app and operating-system versions and the screen you were on, plus two lines the app adds: one about your data folder (how quickly it responds, and whether it sits in Dropbox, OneDrive, Google Drive, iCloud Drive or another sync service) and one counting the sign-ins started and finished on that computer. A report sent while you're signed in is linked to your account. One sent while you're signed out carries no account, only the reply address you type, if you type one; our server uses your IP address, in memory only, to limit how many of those can be sent.

The app checks your subscription with our server when it starts and after you sign in, and every few seconds while it waits for a payment to go through. Each check is tied to your account: it carries your account identifier and the app version, and it does not carry your invoices, your clients or how many invoices you've made — the free plan is counted on your own computer.

3. Our website

freelance-easy.com runs no analytics today. When we turn them on, we will use Plausible Analytics, a cookieless, privacy-focused service. It counts which pages are viewed, the referring site or campaign, the browser, operating system and country, and which download button was clicked — in aggregate, so we can see which campaigns bring visitors. It sets no cookies. To tell visits apart within a day it hashes your IP address and browser details into an identifier that is discarded every day; the IP address itself is not stored (see Plausible's data policy). The analytics script will be served through our own domain. We do not use advertising pixels, retargeting, or any third-party tracker, and nothing on this website is linked to what you do inside the app.

4. Why we collect what we collect

  • To let you sign in. Google handles the password; we receive your email and name after you authorize it. The app requests only your basic profile (name, email).
  • To charge your subscription. Stripe handles the payment and your card details; we receive a customer identifier, the promotion code if you used one, and a notification when your subscription changes or a payment goes through. We never see your full card number.
  • To run the service securely. Server logs and server-side crash reports help us find and fix problems and detect abuse.
  • To communicate about your account. Receipts, payment failures, security notices and service announcements go to your account email. Tips or product news only if you opt in: the tips box you see when you set up the app starts unticked. Nothing of the kind is sent today, and every such email would have a one-click unsubscribe.
  • To understand which channels work. Your optional "how did you hear about us" answer, which plan the app showed you first, the checkout and payment records, and the campaign parameters on our website are used only as aggregate counts.

Under the GDPR our lawful bases are performance of contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) for your account and subscription, and legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) for security logs and aggregate analytics.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your account data: for as long as you have an account. To close your account, email privacy@freelance-easy.com from the address you signed in with; we erase it from our live systems and it leaves our hosting provider's short-lived backups on their schedule.
  • Server request logs and server crash reports: kept briefly, then deleted.
  • Your local data (invoices, clients, attachments, backup folder): under your control. We never delete it from your computer.

You are responsible for keeping the invoice records your tax law requires — most jurisdictions want several years of them. Deleting an invoice in the app deletes it from your computer; we never had a copy.

7. International transfers

Our servers are in the United States. If you are outside the U.S., your account data is processed there. We rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, as applicable. Our documentation and support are oriented toward U.S. and Canadian users.

8. Your rights

  • Access and portability — email us and we'll send you a copy of everything we hold about your account. Your invoices and clients are already on your computer, in an ordinary SQLite database and PDF files.
  • Correction — edit anything in the app; for account fields you can't edit, email us.
  • Deletion — delete invoices and clients in the app at any time; email us to close your account.
  • Objection — email privacy@freelance-easy.com to object to any processing.

California (CCPA/CPRA): you also have the right to know the categories of personal information we collect (§2), the right to opt out of sale or sharing (we don't sell or share), and the right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights. Residents of other U.S. states with privacy laws have equivalent rights. EU/UK/EEA: you may also complain to your supervisory authority. Canada: you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner (priv.gc.ca); Quebec residents may complain to the Commission d'accès à l'information. We don't use automated decision-making.

9. Children

Freelance Easy is a business tool and is not intended for anyone under 16. If you believe someone under 16 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

10. Security

  • All traffic between the app, this website and our server uses HTTPS.
  • Subscription and license requests are signed, so a stolen account identifier alone can't be used to act as you.
  • Our hosting provider encrypts database backups at rest.
  • Your local data is stored on your own computer — its security is ultimately in your hands. We recommend full-disk encryption (FileVault on macOS, BitLocker on Windows) and a backup.

If we learn of a personal-data breach affecting you, we will notify you within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

11. Your backup folder (if you use it)

The backup folder is optional and off by default. When on, the app writes copies of your invoice PDFs and attachments to a folder you choose. It's your folder: deleting an invoice in the app does not remove the copy there, and if you chose a synced folder (Dropbox, iCloud Drive, OneDrive), your sync software will carry those files to your cloud account under your terms with that provider.

12. Changes

Material changes are posted here at least 30 days before they take effect, with a notice in the app. Minor edits (typos, an address, a replacement provider of the same kind) happen without notice.
