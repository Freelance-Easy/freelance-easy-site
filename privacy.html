Privacy policy.

A real privacy policy will be published with the public launch.

Short version while we're in beta: Freelance Easy stores your invoices, clients, and settings on your own machine. We don't receive or host your invoice database, and we don't sell or share personal information. When you sign in, we keep your Google account email and your subscription status so the app can check your license with our server once per launch. Payments are handled by Stripe. Invoices leave your machine only when you email them to a client from your own email account, and a bug report reaches us only if you choose to send one.

Questions? Privacy-specific concerns go to privacy@freelance-easy.com. Everything else, support@freelance-easy.com.
