How to buy

  1. Download the app.

    Download for Mac Download for Windows

    For Apple Silicon Macs (M1 or later) and Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Intel Macs aren't supported. The Windows installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows shows a security warning when you run it, and some Windows 11 PCs with Smart App Control block it outright: what to do.

  2. Open it and sign in with Google. Your seven-day trial starts the first time you sign in, one trial per account.

  3. To subscribe, click See plans in the app, then Continue monthly or Continue yearly. Checkout opens in your browser.

Paying

  • Payment is handled by Stripe; checkout shows the seller.
  • Prices are set in US dollars. Checkout shows the currency and the full total, including any tax, before you pay.
  • Have a promotion code? There is a field for it at checkout.

Before you buy

  • After the trial, these need a subscription: Reports, recurring invoices, sending email from the app, your logo and colors, all four templates, documents, automatic backups, your own invoice numbers and default notes.
  • Opening and downloading your invoices isn't a Pro feature: the free plan and a lapsed subscription can do both.
  • One person per account. If more people in your business need the app, each needs their own account.
  • You can install it on the computers you own or control.
  • The app checks your subscription when it starts. A subscribed app keeps working offline for at least seven days after its last successful check, then asks you to sign in again; your files stay on your computer.
  • There's no minimum term.

Cancelling, refunds and price changes

  • You can also cancel by emailing support@freelance-easy.com, or from your Link account at link.com if checkout named Link as the seller.
  • Cancelling stops the next renewal. When the period you've paid for ends, the app turns read-only.
  • Cancelling doesn't refund the rest of a period you've paid for, except as the Terms describe.
  • If we change the price of your plan, we'll email you between 14 and 30 days before your first renewal at the new price.

The full rules are in the Terms: §2 for the trial and plans, §7 for payment, cancelling and refunds.

Clients, invoices and amounts in the demos, screenshots and tour are fictional.
